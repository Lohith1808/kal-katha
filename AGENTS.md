# History Game Development Rules

1. Never invent historical events, dates, people, locations or outcomes.
2. Never alter the documented historical timeline.
3. Historical content must come from the project's verified content files.
4. Never generate historical facts automatically during gameplay.
5. Gameplay may simplify representation but must not claim invented events are historical facts.
6. Creative/alternate-history mode is not currently implemented.
7. Gameplay failure must reset to a checkpoint rather than create an alternate timeline.
8. Keep historical content separate from gameplay code.
9. Ancient, Medieval and Modern history must remain separately categorized.
10. Every historical chapter should support source references.
11. Do not remove existing functionality without explicit approval.
12. Before major architectural changes, explain the proposed change.
